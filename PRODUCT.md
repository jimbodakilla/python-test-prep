# Product

## Register

product

## Users

6th graders (11-12) learning Python, mostly on school Chromebooks and phones, often the night before a test. Two goals in one product: pass a test on print / input / int / float in about two days, then keep learning (decisions, loops, strings, lists, functions). They are capable, easily bored, quick to reject anything that feels babyish, and anxious about the test. A parent or teacher sometimes looks over their shoulder.

## Product Purpose

An interactive practice site that makes the four basics automatic through live code, immediate honest feedback, and a lot of varied practice, then opens a "Level Up" path for kids who want more. Success: a kid finishes a session feeling "I get it" and wants one more round; the mock test score goes up; wrong answers feel like useful information, not failure.

## Brand Personality

Bold, confident, playful. A friendly small mascot who cheers and encourages. Real game feel (levels, badges, streaks, short celebrations) that rewards effort and accuracy without pressure. Voice: short, warm, direct, a little funny, never talking down.

## Anti-references

- Babyish cartoon-clipart: primary-colour kindergarten look, comic fonts, stock cartoon art.
- Generic school-software gray: dull LMS panels, tiny text, no personality.
- Neon gamer / casino: flashing, loud, slot-machine rewards that pressure kids.
- Corporate SaaS template: white cards, identical icon grids, indigo-gradient hero.

## Design Principles

1. **Code is the hero.** Editors, terminals, trace tables and variable boxes are the most polished things on screen; chrome stays quiet around them.
2. **Wrong answers are information.** Feedback explains why in plain words and invites another try; mistakes never feel punishing.
3. **Earned celebration, never pressure.** Rewards track real mastery and effort. Small wins get small cheers; milestones get a moment. No streak-guilt, no countdown nagging outside the mock test.
4. **Bold, then calm.** Saturated colour and chunky, tactile controls give energy; generous space and clear hierarchy keep a long study session comfortable.
5. **One friendly voice.** The mascot and the copy share a personality that is confident, warm and brief.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard use, screen-reader labels (currently zero axe violations; keep it so). Reduced motion respected everywhere: every celebration and mascot animation has a still alternative. Never use colour alone for right/wrong or types (always icon or label too). Touch targets at least 44px; 16px minimum editor font on touch devices.
